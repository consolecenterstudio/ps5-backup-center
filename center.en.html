Future project

PS5 Center

PS5 Center is the future hub for broader console management: games, applications and interactions between several devices. Its direction includes launching and closing content, coordinating consoles and linking specialised tools. Its scope will be developed with the product family.

A broader console hub

The direction goes beyond a game list: coordinate consoles, manage the content and actions around them, and connect the specialised tools in the family.

Directions to explore

Launching and closing content, managing several consoles, linking remote interactions and coordinating updates are part of the vision to develop. Detailed capabilities and compatibility will be defined with the project.

PS5 Backup Center today

Backup Center already focuses on preserving, comparing, restoring and exchanging content. Its existing game-transfer features stand on their own; this future hub will have its own scope.
