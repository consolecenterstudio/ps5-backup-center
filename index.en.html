PS5 Backup Center

Your collection. Your progress. Your backups.

Back up, compare, restore, transfer and synchronise the content around your PS5: games, apps, ROMs, save copies and active progress. A controller interface and a browser interface, powered by one background engine. Free software, without a subscription.

Home and the notification centre

Home turns the console inventory into actions to consider: games without a known backup, newer copies, duplicates, comparisons to run, items to check, device sync and ongoing tasks. The To do cards appear according to the current inventory and device state.

Follow transfers from Home · The notification shows progress. Open Tasks for details. Updates between consoles also have a Home notification in the workflow being tested.

Library

Find PS5 and PS4 games, apps and emulator ROMs in one visual library. Filter by type, emulator or location, search for a title and open its details to inspect components, storage and compatibility before acting.

Guides →

Progress

Follow each player’s active progress on the console and the supported Switch or emulator profiles. Compare the progress and play time a game records, and select the intended receiving account when you restore or exchange a save.

Guides →

Saves

Keep dated stored copies separately from playable progress. Inspect every version and its location, compare contents, protect important copies and review a cleanup proposal before deleting or restoring anything.

Guides →

Tasks

Use a plan to repeat a selected backup without rebuilding its choices each time. TV and web: Tasks, open the plan editor; a game's details also offers Scheduled plans…. A recurring backup plan is distinct from the future automatic sync triggered by closing a game.

Guides →

Sync

Choose the device, then the content to keep aligned: stored save copies, active progress, apps, supported settings and DLC inventory. Review the proposed direction and accounts before accepting changes.

Guides →

Transfers

Choose the source, destination and operation for a whole game. Copy keeps the source; Move removes it after verification. Review format and receiving-console compatibility, and follow all task phases through verification and installation.

From home to the cabin · On your local network, send a game or a backup copy to a paired PS5. The same workflow between two locations is being tested over the Internet. Use Transfers for whole games and Sync to compare DLC and game updates.

Guides →

Devices

Choose storage and devices in one place: internal and USB disks, supported NAS connections, paired PS5s and Switch consoles. Inspect free space, test a NAS connection and verify the receiving console before sending content.

Switch and network storage · Exchange Switch save data both ways with Eden or ProsperoEden on PS5. Switch ↔ Switch sync is planned. FTP and FTPS are supported. SMB for Windows, Synology and QNAP shares and for reading Switch saves is in testing. SFTP, NFS, rsync, WebDAV and cloud connectors are planned.

Settings

Choose language, theme, sounds and music; review emulator sources, ignored items, users and paired devices. Updates, release notes, diagnostics and component credits are collected here as well.

TV · Web · Engine

The engine runs on the console and serves the web interface as well as backup work. Closing the TV app leaves the engine available. Use your paired browser to browse content and follow Tasks; switching interfaces does not create another backup engine.

Startup and updates · The engine can start with the console through Payload Manager. Once running, it keeps tasks and the web app active. Sending a Backup Center update to another PS5 and fetching it from that PS5 are in testing.
