# Restore a selected save

## What changes

Restoring puts a stored save back into active progress for a receiving account. This is different from installing a whole game or restoring a full-console backup.

## Where to find it

TV: **Saves › All versions**, choose a version and **Restore**. Web: **Saves**, open the game's versions, select the copy and **Restore**. Both interfaces ask you to review the target before writing.

## Before you start

Close the target game. Identify the receiving account and keep an independent copy of its current progress. Check the stored copy's game, content, source and required components. Re-signing for an account does not convert an incompatible save between games, regions or generations.

## Step by step

1. Compare versions and select the playthrough you actually want to resume. Do not select solely by date.
2. Choose **Restore** and the receiving account. Check this again when accounts share a similar display name.
3. Read the verification result. If it fails, stop and record the message; do not force a write on the assumption that the preview is only a warning.
4. Review the proposed safety copy of existing progress and the overwrite scope. Cancel here if the source or account is wrong.
5. Confirm, then follow the restoration in **Tasks** until the final result.
6. Inspect **Progress** for the receiving account. When safe, launch the correct game and check the expected progress. Do not overwrite the independent original copy until that check is satisfactory.

## Another account or console

Account IDs may differ between consoles. Select the intended receiving account rather than relying on matching names. The app applies its account-mapping and re-signing flow; it still requires your target choice and confirmation. Sending a save to a paired PS5 is separate from the future full-console restore wizard.

## If the result is not what you expected

Stop playing so that further autosaves do not complicate recovery. Read the completed task and identify the safety copy and original stored version. Review the version list before restoring again. For a report, share the exact error and sanitized version information, never account IDs or save contents without review.
