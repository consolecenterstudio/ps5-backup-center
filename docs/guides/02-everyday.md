# Everyday backups and restores

Find a title, protect progress and recover a selected version.

## Where to find it

TV: Home / Library / Progress / Saves / Tasks. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

## Steps

1. Filter Library by type, emulator or location; open the item details. Progress shows the playable save on the console. Saves shows stored copies, not playable versions.
2. Choose the account and components to back up; check the destination and free space. Track the operation in Tasks.
3. In Saves, open all versions and compare content, progress and play time. A newer date alone does not prove more advanced progress.

4. For restoration, close the game, select the stored version and receiving account, read verification and safety-copy information, then confirm.
5. For cleanup, inspect the proposed list. Keep protected copies and other users’ copies. Deletion requires a five-second hold; inspect the trash before relying on recovery.
6. For a plan, choose games or rules, components, destination, schedule and versions retained. Save it, then inspect it in Tasks.

## Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.
