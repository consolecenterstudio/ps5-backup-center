# Troubleshooting

Diagnose a problem without losing the evidence.

## Where to find it

TV: Tasks; Settings › Diagnostic and about. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

## Steps

1. Record app and engine versions, the failed action and its exact message. Check Tasks before retrying to avoid a duplicate operation.
2. If the engine is unavailable, distinguish a closed TV app from an unreachable engine. Check the address shown on the console and local-network connectivity.
3. For a NAS, test the configured protocol, credentials and folder permissions. FTP passive data ports may differ from the control port. Do not switch protocols blindly.
4. If a Switch is absent, check ftpd, its displayed address / port and the JKSV export. If Sync remains reading, record the version and diagnostic before relaunching.
5. Before sharing logs or screenshots, remove account IDs, names, addresses, hostnames, passwords, tokens and pairing codes. For security issues follow the private reporting policy.

## Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.
