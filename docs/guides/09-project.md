# Project, roadmap and support

Understand the product family and ways to contribute.

## Where to find it

TV: Settings › What’s new / Acknowledgements. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

## Steps

1. PS5 Backup Center is the current product: a background engine, controller interface and browser interface. It is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later, without a subscription.
2. PS5 Center is a future broader console-management hub. PS5 Connector is a future concept for extending capabilities through connectors. Neither is a current Backup Center feature.
3. Coming work includes SFTP, NAS trash, remote fleet updates, PS5 DLC installation, Orbit / PatchDL bridges, incoming Orbit / BlackBox game workflows, Switch-to-Switch sync, automatic sync and PS4 Backup Center.
4. Read the changelog and contribution / security policies. Support links and creator contact channels will be chosen before publication; no donation account or payment destination is active yet.

## Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.
