PS5 Backup Center

PS5 Backup Center · Privacy policy

PS5 Backup Center is an application from Console Center Studio that runs on your PlayStation 5. You can contact us at consolecenterstudio@gmail.com for questions about this policy or how the app uses your Google data.

Connecting Google Drive

Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, your PS5 displays a code. You enter that code and sign in on Google's website using your own Google account. Google handles your sign-in and password.

The app requests Google's `drive.file` permission. It uses this permission to create and manage the backup files it can access, including the information needed to list, locate and restore those files. The app may read account information returned by Drive to identify the connected account in the app.

Storage and use

The app sends the backup files you choose to store in Drive from your console to your own Google Drive. Restoring a backup downloads the selected files from Drive to your console. The app stores a Google authorization token in the console's app configuration so it can reconnect when needed. Console Center Studio does not receive your backup files or OAuth tokens through this integration.

PS5 Backup Center's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The integration uses Google data for the backup and restore features you choose. Console Center Studio does not sell that data or use it for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting backups

You can revoke access in your Google Account connections. You can also remove the Drive destination from PS5 Backup Center. To delete backups already stored in Drive, manage those files in your own Google Drive. Revoking access does not delete stored backups.
