PS5 Connector

PS5 Connector explores extending console capabilities through connectors to existing resources and services. External storage, network shares and NAS are examples of that direction. It is a future concept whose design and scope remain to be developed.

Extend the resources around a console

Explore how existing resources and services could be connected and presented to a console through dedicated connectors. NAS, network shares and external media illustrate the direction.

A concept to develop

The connector types, how resources are presented and the supported environments remain to be designed. No release date or deployable prototype is announced.

Separate from current backup destinations

Backup Center has its own supported storage connections. This concept is a broader extension project, with a separate scope to define.
