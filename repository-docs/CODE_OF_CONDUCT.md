# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want PS5 Center to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to homebrew.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Help others keep their data safe: no advice that risks someone's saves without saying so.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, console IDs, account names) without permission.
- Links to pirated content or requests for it. Issues and discussions asking where to download games will be closed.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the private contact channel selected for the release. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
