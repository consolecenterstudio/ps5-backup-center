# Product family

## PS5 Backup Center

The current product brings backups, comparison, restoration, sync and transfers together through its background engine, TV interface and browser interface.

## PS5 Center — future project

A broader console-management hub for games, applications and interactions across devices. The scope will be developed separately from Backup Center.

## PS5 Connector — future concept

Explore extending console capabilities through connectors to existing resources and services. NAS, network shares and external storage are examples of that direction.

## PS4 Backup Center — future project

A future member of the family, with its own compatibility and release scope to define.
