Start here

Install, pair a browser and make your first stored copy.

Where to find it

TV: Settings › Devices; Library. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

Steps

  1. Use a PS5 already configured for compatible native homebrew. Obtain the release package and compare its SHA-256 with the release checksum before installing. Public download and store links will accompany the release.
  2. Launch PS5 Backup Center. Wait for the engine connection; continuing without it does not start backup work.
  1. In Settings › Devices, read the web address shown by your console. Open it on your local network and enter the six-digit pairing code.
  1. Open Library, choose a game, open its details and select its save component and a destination. Review the summary before confirming.
  2. Open Tasks and check the result. Open Saves to find the dated copy.

Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.

Home and the notification centre

Home turns the console inventory into actions to consider: games without a known backup, newer copies, duplicates, comparisons to run, items to check, device sync and ongoing tasks. The To do cards appear according to the current inventory and device state.

Where to find it

TV: Home, then move down from the featured title to To do and the shelves below. Web: Home, scroll through the same sections and open the relevant card. TV uses controller navigation; the browser uses selectable cards and buttons.

Read the notification before acting

Card or state What to check next
To back up Which game and player's progress lack a known copy on the available storage?
Newer copy Compare the candidate's content, progress and account before restoring.
Duplicates Read the cleanup proposal and the versions that will remain.
Not yet compared Run or await content comparison; dates alone do not establish duplication.
To check Inspect the copy and the reported issue before deleting or overwriting it.
Sync · device Check the selected games, direction and conflicts for that device.
Task progress Read the operation, percentage and estimated time, then open Tasks for the result.

The cards use category colours and covers to distinguish work. A count describes the affected selection; it is not a promise that every game on every disk has been synchronised. “No games chosen” is a selection state, not a sync failure. “Everything is in sync” concerns the selected scope.

Step by step

  1. Review the featured title and To do cards before starting another task.
  2. Open the relevant card. Check the affected game, player and storage rather than accepting an action from the count alone.
  3. Compare or select the intended content, read the summary and confirmations, then start only the intended operation.
  4. Follow Tasks to the final result. Return to Home and allow the inventory and notification state to refresh.
  5. Scroll further for recently played titles, PS5/PS4 content, saves without an installed game, content available elsewhere, retro platforms and applications. Shelves depend on what is found.

Use the right space

Library answers what content is available; Progress shows the playable save by player; Saves manages stored copies. Sync aligns selected content across devices, Transfers handles whole games, Devices configures destinations and peers, Tasks tracks work and plans, and Settings manages language, themes, sources, users, updates and credits. Home links these spaces through the notifications; opening a card is not itself proof of a completed operation.

Everyday backups and restores

Find a title, protect progress and recover a selected version.

Where to find it

TV: Home / Library / Progress / Saves / Tasks. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

Steps

  1. Filter Library by type, emulator or location; open the item details. Progress shows the playable save on the console. Saves shows stored copies, not playable versions.

  2. Choose the account and components to back up; check the destination and free space. Track the operation in Tasks.

  3. In Saves, open all versions and compare content, progress and play time. A newer date alone does not prove more advanced progress.

  4. For restoration, close the game, select the stored version and receiving account, read verification and safety-copy information, then confirm.

  5. For cleanup, inspect the proposed list. Keep protected copies and other users’ copies. Deletion requires a five-second hold; inspect the trash before relying on recovery.

  6. For a plan, choose games or rules, components, destination, schedule and versions retained. Save it, then inspect it in Tasks.

Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.

Make and verify a backup

What you will get

A dated stored copy of the components you choose. Backing up only a save does not copy the game. A full game copy and a save copy serve different purposes; neither should be mistaken for the active progress used when you launch a game.

Where to find it

TV: Library, open a title; for a player's progress use Progress. Navigate tabs with L1/R1. Web: open the corresponding Library or Progress tab and select the same item. Follow the operation in Tasks on either interface.

Before you start

Connect the destination, check its identity and free space, and close the game concerned. If there are several accounts, identify the player whose progress you intend to protect. The same game can have different saves for different players.

Step by step

  1. Open the title's details and review its platform, location and offered components.
  2. Select the required components: save, game, update, DLC, emulator state or app configuration, as offered for this item. Do not assume all components exist for every title.
  3. Choose the destination explicitly. Read the actual disk or device name; USB numbering alone does not identify a drive permanently.
  4. Review size, component selection and destination. Choose Back up only when the summary matches your intention.
  5. Open Tasks. Read the final result, including errors or skipped items. Closing the TV interface is different from stopping the engine or turning off the PS5.
  6. Open Saves, select the game and All versions. Find the dated copy, its storage location and the recorded components. A task submission alone is not the result you are checking.

Check the result

The task should report completion and the expected stored version should be listed. File verification checks the copy; it does not establish that every future game version can read a restored save. Keep an independent copy of important progress.

If it stops

Read the task's error first. Reconnect the same destination and inspect the existing task before starting another one. The engine journals work for resumption; do not delete partial files or rename the backup tree to force a retry.

Compare copies and clean up duplicates

What it is for

Keep useful versions while avoiding multiple identical copies on the same storage. A content fingerprint identifies identical data; the date alone cannot tell which copy contains the most advanced playthrough.

Where to find it

TV: Saves, choose the game and All versions. Web: Saves, choose the same game and open its versions. On TV, L2 selects copies and Options opens actions for the selection; on the web use checkboxes and the action menu.

Understand the list

  • Location tells you where the copy is stored.
  • Different content letters distinguish different contents in this comparison; they are not a ranking from best to worst.
  • Progress and play time depend on what the game records and what can be read. A missing value is not evidence of an empty save.
  • Protection and account indicators matter before any cleanup. A copy belonging to another player is not a spare version of your own progress.

Step by step

  1. Open the versions and review every storage location, scrolling to the bottom.
  2. Request content comparison when available; let the background comparison finish with the game closed.
  3. Separate identical copies from genuinely different progress. Decide which versions you want to keep before choosing cleanup.
  4. Protect the versions you want preserved. Keeping a recent set of versions is not the same as keeping every distinct playthrough.
  5. Open the cleanup proposal and inspect each selected copy. Check account, storage, protected versions and the copies that will remain.
  6. Cancel if the proposal does not match your intention. To accept deletion, read the confirmation and complete the requested five-second hold.
  7. Reopen the versions and inspect the trash where supported. Check the displayed recovery period; local trash uses 30 days. Do not assume the same recovery is available for every remote storage operation.

What to avoid

Do not delete solely because two copies have the same timestamp. Do not remove the last independent copy of a distinct progress state. NAS-copy trash is future work; do not treat NAS deletion as recoverable through a local trash.

Restore a selected save

What changes

Restoring puts a stored save back into active progress for a receiving account. This is different from installing a whole game or restoring a full-console backup.

Where to find it

TV: Saves › All versions, choose a version and Restore. Web: Saves, open the game's versions, select the copy and Restore. Both interfaces ask you to review the target before writing.

Before you start

Close the target game. Identify the receiving account and keep an independent copy of its current progress. Check the stored copy's game, content, source and required components. Re-signing for an account does not convert an incompatible save between games, regions or generations.

Step by step

  1. Compare versions and select the playthrough you actually want to resume. Do not select solely by date.
  2. Choose Restore and the receiving account. Check this again when accounts share a similar display name.
  3. Read the verification result. If it fails, stop and record the message; do not force a write on the assumption that the preview is only a warning.
  4. Review the proposed safety copy of existing progress and the overwrite scope. Cancel here if the source or account is wrong.
  5. Confirm, then follow the restoration in Tasks until the final result.
  6. Inspect Progress for the receiving account. When safe, launch the correct game and check the expected progress. Do not overwrite the independent original copy until that check is satisfactory.

Another account or console

Account IDs may differ between consoles. Select the intended receiving account rather than relying on matching names. The app applies its account-mapping and re-signing flow; it still requires your target choice and confirmation. Sending a save to a paired PS5 is separate from the future full-console restore wizard.

If the result is not what you expected

Stop playing so that further autosaves do not complicate recovery. Read the completed task and identify the safety copy and original stored version. Review the version list before restoring again. For a report, share the exact error and sanitized version information, never account IDs or save contents without review.

Schedule a backup plan

Purpose and access

Use a plan to repeat a selected backup without rebuilding its choices each time. TV and web: Tasks, open the plan editor; a game's details also offers Scheduled plans…. A recurring backup plan is distinct from the future automatic sync triggered by closing a game.

Prepare the plan

  1. Choose a preset if its scope matches your goal, or start a custom plan.
  2. Give the plan a recognizable name. Choose individual games or the offered selection rules; review how new games are included.
  3. Choose the components. A save-only plan does not protect the game files; an app-only plan does not necessarily contain its configuration.
  4. Choose the destination and confirm the disk identity and free space. The destination must be accessible when the plan runs.
  5. Choose on-demand, daily or the offered weekdays and time. Check the displayed schedule rather than assuming that closing the TV app stops it.
  6. Set the number of versions to keep and review the retention scope. Rotation is not a substitute for an independent long-term copy.
  7. Save the plan and reopen it to check the persisted choices. If appropriate, run it once on demand and inspect the actual task result.

Follow and change it

Open Tasks to distinguish a saved plan from a queued, running, completed or failed execution. To change the scope, edit the plan, save and reopen it. Pause the plan before making destination changes if you do not want it to run meanwhile. Cancelling an execution and pausing a recurring plan are different decisions.

Check the result

Inspect the task report and the expected versions in Saves. A plan appearing in the editor does not establish that a scheduled execution happened. If a destination was offline, read the error and existing queue before requesting a new run.

Destinations and linked consoles

Choose storage and connect a second console or a Switch.

Where to find it

TV: Devices; Sync. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

Connect a Switch or NAS · In Progress, compare JKSV saves with Eden or ProsperoEden, then choose the profile and direction, Switch → PS5 or PS5 → Switch. SMB for NAS connections and for reading JKSV on a Switch is in testing.

Steps

  1. For USB, select the identified disk and verify available space. Use its displayed identity rather than assuming its port number stays the same.
  2. For a NAS, add the device, choose the supported connection type, enter its connection details, test, browse and select the destination folder before saving. FTP and FTPS are supported; SMB is being developed.
  3. To link two PS5s, open Devices on both, select the discovered console or enter its address, then confirm the pairing code. Inspect the receiving console and disk before sending.
  4. For Switch saves, start ftpd and prepare a JKSV export. Select the device and match the game and Eden / ProsperoEden profile. After sending back, follow the JKSV import instructions.
  5. In Sync choose a device, then Saves, Active progress, Apps, DLC or Settings. Review direction and conflicts before confirming. Whole games opens Transfers. NAS depot mode concerns active progress and apps; DLC and settings are PS5-to-PS5.

Safety and attention

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.

Choose the right sync workflow

Choose what should match

Section What is synchronised What to check
Saves Stored copies Selection, versions and destination storage
Active progress The progress used to resume play Receiving accounts, direction and conflicts
Apps App versions Version, fingerprint and whether the app is closed
Settings App configuration according to its profile App only, app with settings, or settings only
DLC Inventory and supported missing-component transfers Platform, base game and supported operation

Whole games belong in Transfers. Keeping stored save copies on both devices does not itself replace playable progress.

Where to find it

TV: Sync, choose the device at left and open the relevant section. Web: Sync, select the same device and section. Inspect Tasks for execution and results.

Two PS5 consoles

  1. Pair the consoles through Devices. Check which console is local and which is receiving.
  2. Open Sync, select the other PS5, then choose one section. Start with a small, explicit selection.
  3. For stored copies, review which games and versions are included and whether newly discovered games should be added automatically. That selection option is different from automatic unattended sync, which is future work.
  4. For active progress, verify the account pair, direction, progress and play time. Close the game on both consoles. Where both sides changed, choose the intended progress rather than assuming the date resolves the conflict.
  5. For apps, close the target app and review versions and fingerprints. Equal version labels with different contents need a decision. Select the settings scope supported by that app's profile.
  6. Read the proposed changes, choose Sync now when appropriate, and follow Tasks. Reopen the comparison to inspect the resulting state.

NAS depot

Choose the NAS in Sync. Stored copies, active progress and apps are separate sections. For active progress, the depot holds the chosen stored version used to bring consoles up to level; it does not run the game on the NAS. For apps, it holds app versions for installation on a console. Check direction before accepting a local replacement. DLC and app-settings synchronisation in this view remain PS5-to-PS5 workflows.

Switch and Eden / ProsperoEden

Prepare JKSV exports and start ftpd on the Switch. Open the Switch device in Sync, check the game and profile on each side, review the comparison and direction, then confirm. When sending back to the Switch, complete the JKSV import steps: copying an export is not the same as applying it to the active Switch save. Direct Switch-to-Switch sync is future work.

DLC and packages

An inventory or a discovered .pkg is not an installation. Switch add-on transfers between PS5s concern supported files missing from the receiving game's emulator folder. PS5 DLC installation through an installer and Orbit / PatchDL bridges are future work.

Refresh and recovery

Prepared plans make the comparison appear quickly; they do not mean continuous automatic sync. If a device is offline, reconnect and refresh before accepting an old proposal. After interruption, inspect Tasks and the refreshed comparison before retrying.

Copy or move a whole game

Choose the operation

Copy retains the source. Move removes the source after the destination copy has been verified. Compression changes the representation of the game; it does not back up active progress by itself. Check save components separately.

Where to find it

TV: Transfers, or open a game's details in Library. Web: Transfers, with the same source, destination and operation choices. Progress and errors appear in Tasks.

Send to another PS5 · On TV or web, select both paired consoles in Transfers and choose Copy. Check the game and direction, then follow the task in Home or Tasks. For a PS5 away from home, Devices → Remote PS5 opens the Internet workflow being tested.

Step by step

  1. Close the game on the source and receiving console. If the title is running, stop here instead of working around the refusal.
  2. Select the source device and actual storage disk. Select the destination separately and check available space.
  3. Choose Copy or Move, then the title. Review included data; a game, its DLC and its player saves are distinct components.
  4. If a format choice is offered, check the receiving system's support for folder, .exfat or .ffpfsc. Review minimum system, SDK and backport evidence. A warning is not resolved by choosing a different filename.
  5. For another PS5, verify the receiving disk and account. Review the final summary and start the task only when direction and scope are correct.
  6. Wait for copy verification and installation / registration steps to finish. Do not unplug a disk simply because the copying percentage reached 100%.
  7. Read the final task result. Refresh the receiving Library and check location and format. A completed transfer is distinct from a successful launch; test the title when appropriate.

Image tools

Use the dedicated tools for compression, decompression, extraction, verification, AMPR indexing or read-speed measurement. Read the explanation for the selected tool: inspection and repair do not have the same effects. Do not change read-only settings or run repair to compensate for an unrelated transfer error.

If a move fails

Keep both storage devices connected. Read which phase failed before deleting anything. Do not manually erase the source on the assumption that the destination is complete. Record the task's error and inspect the destination before deciding on recovery.

Different operation: full PS5 backup

The full-backup checklist groups account, save, app, registration, capture and startup data according to the offered categories. It is not a bootable clone or the future full-console restore wizard. Review the category list and destination independently of a game transfer.

Use the web app and background engine

Three parts, one engine

The TV app is the controller interface. The web app is the browser interface. The background engine serves the web pages and performs scans, backups, transfers and task tracking. Closing either interface does not itself stop the engine. Turning off the console or stopping its engine is different.

Pair and start

  1. On TV open Settings › Devices. Read the current web address and pairing code on your own console.
  2. Open that address on a phone, tablet or computer on the same local network. Do not use an address copied from a screenshot or another installation.
  3. Enter the current six-digit code and a recognizable browser/device name, then pair. Treat pairing as granting that browser access to the app's data and commands.
  4. Open Library and inspect the filters. Open a title to check its components, then inspect Tasks. The web interface uses buttons and checkboxes rather than controller shortcuts.
  5. Start an operation only after reviewing its destination and scope. Follow its final result in Tasks. Leaving the browser tab or TV app is not the same as cancelling the task.

Return later

Reopen the console's current address. A previously paired browser can retain its association; another browser or cleared browser storage may require pairing again. After an update, allow the interface to reload, then verify the app/engine version if the displayed information seems stale.

If disconnected

Check that console and engine are still running and the device is on the intended local network. Read the console's current address again. Inspect existing Tasks before restarting an operation. Do not publish the address, pairing code or browser token in a bug report, and do not forward the engine's local port to the internet.

Operating boundaries

A responsive web page does not prove that a particular backup completed. Conversely, closing a TV window does not prove the engine stopped. Use the task report and resulting version to confirm an operation. A mobile browser adapts its layout; controls and paths remain the same concepts as the TV interface.

Troubleshooting

Diagnose a problem without losing the evidence.

Where to find it

TV: Tasks; Settings › Diagnostic and about. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

Steps

  1. Record app and engine versions, the failed action and its exact message. Check Tasks before retrying to avoid a duplicate operation.
  2. If the engine is unavailable, distinguish a closed TV app from an unreachable engine. Check the address shown on the console and local-network connectivity.
  3. For a NAS, test the configured protocol, credentials and folder permissions. FTP passive data ports may differ from the control port. Do not switch protocols blindly.
  4. If a Switch is absent, check ftpd, its displayed address / port and the JKSV export. If Sync remains reading, record the version and diagnostic before relaunching.
  5. Before sharing logs or screenshots, remove account IDs, names, addresses, hostnames, passwords, tokens and pairing codes. For security issues follow the private reporting policy.

Close the affected game or app before restoring, replacing or moving it. Check account, direction and destination; read confirmations and keep an independent copy of important data. Verification and safety copies reduce risk without providing an absolute guarantee.

Project, roadmap and support

Understand the product family and ways to contribute.

Where to find it

TV: Settings › What’s new / Acknowledgements. Use L1/R1 for tabs; open the gear for Settings.

Web: the corresponding spaces in the paired browser navigation; use buttons and checkboxes.

Steps

  1. PS5 Backup Center is the current product: a background engine, controller interface and browser interface. It is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later, without a subscription.
  2. PS5 Center is a future broader console-management hub. PS5 Connector is a future concept for extending capabilities through connectors. Neither is a current Backup Center feature.
  3. Coming work includes SFTP, NAS trash, remote fleet updates, PS5 DLC installation, Orbit / PatchDL bridges, incoming Orbit / BlackBox game workflows, Switch-to-Switch sync, automatic sync and PS4 Backup Center.
